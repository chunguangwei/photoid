## 简体中文

名称：PhotoID Studio
副标题：挑选原图，制作证件照

宣传文本：
选好证件照尺寸，从相册的一批照片或指定文件夹里挑合适的原图，再完成裁切、换底和文件大小调整。人工选图与拍照仍可直接使用。

描述：
PhotoID Studio 帮你从已有照片中找到适合制作的原图，并按所选尺寸在本机准备照片文件。

• 先选尺寸：从 33 个常用规格中选择，或手动填写像素、KB 和底色要求。
• 批量找照片：多选相册照片，或选择文件夹；本机检测人脸，并按所选尺寸查看裁剪像素与画质参考。
• 看图再决定：优先查看推荐，也能浏览全部照片。点开预览，按需查看理由，由你决定用哪张。
• 直接制作：采用后进入原有编辑器，调整裁剪、底色和 JPEG 大小。也可人工选一张或直接拍照。
• 加强版（可选）：开启后用本机语义模型按一句话描述重排当前批次照片，模型经你确认才下载、可随时删除，照片与查询不上传。
• 保留制作记录：可继续制作、检查实际文件尺寸与字节，使用已有多份文件、要求截图识别和交付工具。

品牌加载动效轻量呈现，可立即进入首页并支持减少动态效果。

照片不为模型处理上传。系统相册和文件提供方可能下载你明确选择的内容；第三方 SDK 技术诊断见隐私政策。批量数量、文件大小和格式有限制，相册多选手势以系统支持为准。

筛选结果仅供制作参考，可能漏检或误判，不识别身份，不构成合规认证或受理保证。请核对接收方最新要求。本应用与政府机构无关，不签发身份证件，不保证任何机构接收照片。

关键词：证件照,照片,尺寸,裁剪,压缩,换底,相册,文件夹,本地AI,像素,报名

更新说明：
• 简化找照片流程：选尺寸、选一批照片、点一张开始制作。
• 支持 33 个常用规格及手动补充要求。
• 新增系统相册批量选择，保留目录、多文件、人工选图和拍照。
• 移除示例入口，折叠技术说明，让结果网格更直观。
• 换尺寸复用分析，取消换图保留原有结果。
• 保留品牌加载动效、制作记录和文件交付工具。

## English (US)

Name: PhotoID Studio
Subtitle: Find and prepare ID photos

Promotional text:
Choose a size, select a batch of photos or a folder, and find a starting photo to crop, recolor and resize. Manual photo selection and camera capture remain available.

Description:
PhotoID Studio helps you find a portrait to prepare, then create photo files for your chosen size on your device.

• Choose a size: use 33 common specifications or enter custom pixels, KB limits and background requirements.
• Find photos in a batch: select multiple library photos or a folder. On-device face detection and image measurements provide crop-resolution and quality references.
• Review and decide: browse recommended or all photos. Tap to preview, optionally inspect reasons, and choose which photo to use.
• Prepare directly: continue to cropping, backgrounds and JPEG size control. You can also select one photo manually or use the camera.
• Keep preparation history: resume work, inspect actual dimensions and bytes, and use the existing multiple-output, requirement-screenshot recognition and file-delivery tools.

A brief brand intro supports immediate continue and Reduce Motion.

Photos are not uploaded for model processing. System photo/file providers may retrieve content you explicitly select; SDK technical diagnostics are described in the privacy policy. File, format and batch limits apply. Multi-selection gestures depend on the system.

Suggestions may miss faces or misjudge quality. This is not identity recognition, institutional certification or an acceptance guarantee. Check current requirements with the receiving organization. PhotoID Studio is independent of government agencies and does not issue identity documents.

Keywords: portrait,crop,pixels,jpeg,resize,background,photos,folder,local,requirements

What’s New:
• Optional Enhanced finder: rank the current batch by a plain-language query (e.g. "office photo in a business suit") with an on-device semantic model. Photos and queries are never uploaded.
• The ~287 MB model downloads only after your confirmation, with resume, integrity checks and a Wi-Fi-only option; delete it anytime.
• Enhanced ranking is advisory and never changes the existing detection or grouping; turning it off releases the model.
• The app is now named PhotoID; the startup animation is longer and can be skipped anytime.
• Batch library selection, folder scanning, preparation history and delivery tools remain available.
