PhotoID Studio adds a simpler local source-finding flow. No login is required.

1. Open AI photo finder on Home. Choose a size from the 33 common specifications, search by name or pixel dimensions, or enter custom dimensions and KB limits. Common specifications are preparation references, not official certification.
2. Choose multiple photos from the system photo library, or select a downloaded folder. The app analyzes only the chosen scope. File multi-selection and opt-in subfolders are under the additional options.
3. Review the photo grid. Recommended photos appear first; All photos includes uncertain, low-resolution and skipped items with reasons. Tap a photo to preview; detailed measurements are optional. Recommendations are not acceptance guarantees.
4. Tap Prepare with this photo to enter the existing crop/background/JPEG-size editor using the chosen specification. A source-backed preparation record is saved only after explicit adoption. Manual single-photo and camera entry remain available.
5. Preparation history and existing-file checks remain accessible from Home. Existing multi-output requirements, screenshot OCR confirmation and file delivery are preserved as secondary tools, rather than prerequisites for finding a photo.
6. Changing the target size reuses completed detections. Canceling a replacement selection retains the previous batch. Stop and clear controls are available.

There are no user-facing sample-task or sample-photo creation entries. To test, select photos from the review device library or Files. A demonstration recording and final screenshots should show the matching binary; internal engineering fixtures are not presented as an app feature.

Implementation: Apple Vision + scoped PHPicker/document access on iOS; bundled ML Kit + system Photo Picker/SAF on Android. iOS photo providers are read one representation at a time, without copying hundreds of originals up front. Scanning and cache limits apply, and original bytes and saved requirements are checked again before adoption. Photos are not uploaded for model processing; selected providers may download files, and SDK technical diagnostics are disclosed in the privacy policy.

This update changes the working flow beyond styling one portrait: it helps users find a source against a chosen specification and then prepare and inspect actual files. We understand the previous 4.3(a) concern and provide observable behavior rather than relying on implementation originality alone. The app is independent of government agencies, does not issue documents and does not guarantee acceptance.
